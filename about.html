About us

Free image compression for everyday web work.

CompressPixel is a free image compressor designed for people who need smaller images quickly: bloggers, students, developers, shop owners, job applicants, and social media creators.

The tool processes images locally in your browser whenever possible. That means your selected images are not intentionally uploaded to our server for compression. This keeps the experience fast, private, and simple to use.

Our goal

Our goal is to make image optimization easier for everyone. Smaller images can help websites load faster, reduce storage use, improve sharing, and meet upload size limits.

Editorial standards

We aim to publish useful, accurate, and practical guidance about image compression, web performance, and file optimization. Tool recommendations and educational content should be clear, honest, and easy to understand.
