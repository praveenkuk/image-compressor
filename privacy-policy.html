Privacy policy

How CompressPixel handles privacy.

Last updated: May 19, 2026

CompressPixel provides a free browser-based image compression tool. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website.

Image processing

Images selected in the compressor are processed locally in your browser whenever possible. We do not intentionally upload your selected images to our own server for compression.

Information we may collect

We may collect basic usage information such as pages visited, browser type, device type, approximate region, referring pages, and interaction data. This information helps us improve the website and understand traffic.

Cookies and analytics

We may use analytics services such as Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage.

Advertising

We may display ads from Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google may use advertising cookies to show personalized or non-personalized ads depending on user settings and applicable law.

Third-party links

This website may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites.

Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. You can also manage Google advertising preferences through Google's ad settings.
