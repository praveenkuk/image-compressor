Questions

Which format should I use?

JPG for photos you share or upload anywhere. PNG for screenshots, logos and anything that needs transparency or pin-sharp text. WebP or AVIF for websites, where smaller files load faster.

Does converting lose quality?

Converting to PNG is lossless. JPG, WebP and AVIF use the quality slider; 85–90% looks identical to the original for photos.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server, which is faster and keeps private photos and documents private.
