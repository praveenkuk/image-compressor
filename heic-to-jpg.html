Questions

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones and iPads use for photos by default since iOS 11. It saves space, but Windows, many websites and older apps cannot open it.

Why can't I open HEIC photos on Windows?

Windows needs a paid or optional extension to read HEIC. Converting to JPG means the photo opens everywhere, including upload forms that only accept JPG or PNG.

Does converting reduce quality?

At the default 90% JPG quality the difference is not visible. Choose PNG if you need a lossless copy.

Can I stop my iPhone saving HEIC?

Yes. Go to Settings > Camera > Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be JPG, but they take roughly twice the space.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server, which is faster and keeps private photos and documents private.
