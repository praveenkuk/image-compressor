Questions

How do I combine several photos into one PDF?

Add all the photos, put them in order with the arrow buttons, then press Create PDF. Each photo becomes one page.

Can I scan documents with my phone and make a PDF?

Yes. Take a photo of each page, open this page in your phone browser, add the photos and create the PDF.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Choose 'Smallest file' under PDF size. It reduces image resolution and quality enough for email and upload forms while keeping text readable.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server, which is faster and keeps private photos and documents private.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.
